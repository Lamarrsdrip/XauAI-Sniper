## 0. What this audit could and could not use — read this first

The task asked for MT5 Terminal Journal `Print()` logs containing `ADAPTIVE-DIRECTION | HTF Bias: ... | Active Direction: ... | Reason: ...` lines. **I looked for these and they do not exist anywhere on this machine.** Specifics:

- The only terminal instance directory is `Terminal/D0E8209F77C8CF37AD8BF550E51FF075/`. It has no `MQL5/Logs/` directory at all.
- The only `.log` files that exist anywhere under `Terminal/` are tiny (~794–1586 byte) files at `Terminal/D0E8209F77C8CF37AD8BF550E51FF075/temp/logs/YYYYMMDD.log`. I opened several (including `20260707.log`, `20260708`-adjacent dates) — they contain only `LiveUpdate`/`Terminal shutdown` service messages, not EA `Print()` output.
- I also grepped the entire 31MB `XAUAI_TradingIntelligence_XAUUSD.jsonl` (17,004 lines after UTF-16→UTF-8 conversion) for the literal strings `ADAPTIVE-DIRECTION`, `ADAPTIVE_DIRECTION`, `Active Direction`, `HTF Bias`, `HTF bias` — **zero matches for all of them.** The event/reasonKey taxonomy actually present is `BLOCKED`, `BLOCK_CHECK`, `MARKET_SNAPSHOT`, `OPEN`, `CLOSE`, `POST_CLOSE`, `ACCEL_LEARNING`, `EV_POST_CLOSE_REVIEW`, `DATASET_READY`, `STARTUP_SYNC`, plus one `CLOUD_SIGNAL`/`CLOUD_CLOSE` pair.

**Conclusion: there is no "Active Direction" state variable logged anywhere in the data available on this machine.** Whatever internal HTF-bias/active-direction state the EA computes, it is not being written to any file or journal I can read here. This audit therefore cannot literally do step 2 of the task ("find the specific ADAPTIVE-DIRECTION log line"). Instead, I built the closest honest proxy the data supports and I am flagging that substitution explicitly everywhere it matters, rather than presenting a proxy as if it were the real thing:

- **"Bot recognition" proxy** = the timestamp of the first `BLOCKED` event (a real decision cycle that evaluated and rejected a specific direction/setup combination) whose `dir` matches the eventual executed trade's direction, within the lookback window before that trade opened. This is a genuine "the bot considered this direction as a live candidate" signal — it is just not the same thing as an internal "Active Direction" state flip, which may occur earlier or later than the first blocked candidate in that direction.
- **"Earliest valid evidence" proxy** = real M5 OHLC/RSI/stochastic/EMA readings from `MARKET_SNAPSHOT` events (genuine EA-observed prices, not derived), read the same conservative way as the two prior audits: only structure that was already printed/closed at that bar, no forward-looking information.

A second, load-bearing discovery from this exercise, described in full in section 3: **the EA logs zero `MARKET_SNAPSHOT`/`BLOCK_CHECK`/`BLOCKED` events for the entire duration any position is open.** I confirmed this against all 8 executed trades in the window — every single one has exactly 0 intervening scan/block events between its `OPEN` and `CLOSE` timestamps (`openPositions=0` appears in 5,000 of ~4,965+ snapshot rows; `openPositions=1/2/3` appears only ~108 times total, and never inside a single-position hold window in this sample). This is a real, verified data limitation, not a inference from silence — and it turns out to be central to the user's specific complaint (section 2).

### Verdict: was the SELL a "reverse after failure," or independently evidenced?

**Independently evidenced, not a reverse-after-SL rule** — with an important caveat about *why* it looked coincidental:

1. The SELL that executed (`decisionId 9471378302`) is a distinct, freshly-scored A-grade decision cycle, not a re-trade of either of the two specific SELL candidates the EA had been tracking as blocked since 08:28 and 09:10 — and those two tracked candidates were *not* clean wins on the EA's own forward-tracking (`wouldSL1R=Y` for both). There is no text anywhere in the entry reason, `extra`, or reasonKey fields resembling "reverse after SL" or "prior trade failed" logic — the entry reason is generic TREND_PULLBACK/TREND-CONTINUATION boilerplate identical in form to the BUY's.
2. The market's own personality classification flipped from `MOMENTUM_CONT` (at the BUY) to `STRONG_TREND` (at the SELL) — a real, independent state change, not a residual signal from the closed BUY.
3. The suspicious ~92-second gap between SL-hit and SELL-open is fully explained mechanically: the EA does not evaluate *any* candidates while a position is open (§0), so the very first scan cycle after any close will always be within about a minute — this would be true regardless of which direction preceded it, and is not evidence of a deliberate reversal heuristic.
4. However, the *underlying bearish bias* the SELL finally acted on had been visible and repeatedly (if weakly) flagged for at least 2 hours before the BUY was even opened, and arguably since midnight via the EMA structure. The BUY itself was arguably the anomaly: a single overbought spike bar (RSI 60, stoch 83) traded against 9+ hours of prevailing bearish EMA structure, at the exact top of the range (h=4133.61 — the local high referenced in the user's report).
5. The genuine "lateness" in this case is not a gate dwelling too long on the correct SELL — it's that **the EA was completely blind to the market for the entire 40-minute duration of the incorrect BUY**, during which the reversal it should have caught happened out of view. By the time it looked again, the move was already largely finished (RSI 39.5, stoch 11.3 on the very first post-close bar) — and then it exited the correct SELL early anyway, leaving ~$314/8.98 ATR of further favorable movement unclaimed within the same hour.

## 3. Is the delay systemic or trade-specific?

**Mixed, not uniformly "N candles late every time" — but one specific mechanism is present in 100% of the sample and is the more serious finding.**

- Two trades (#2, #5) recognized and fired within 1–2 candles — fast, unremarkable.
- Two trades (#1, #4) show real multi-cycle soft-gate dwell of 6–8 candles, driven by different gates each time (A+ EVIDENCE DEMOTION/BAD-TIMING/TRADE-BRAIN chain for #1; repeated "momentum slowdown 3-bar-range" filter for #4). Trade #1 is notable because the 40-minute wait did **not** prevent a loss — the eventual entry bought into an already-fading bounce and hit its stop.
- Trade #6 shows an architectural anomaly (parallel decisionIds for the same direction/setup evaluated independently — one hard-blocked, a different one executed in the same cycle) that is outside the "gate dwell time" framing entirely.
- Trades #7/#8 (the user's flagged trade) show **zero gate-dwell delay** on the SELL side once the EA could look — 22 seconds. The entire "lateness" here is explained by the position-open scan blackout, confirmed present in **all 8 of 8** sampled trades (100%), which is a different and more structural mechanism than a slow-to-satisfy confirmation gate.

Given the small sample (8 trades, mostly BUY — only 1 SELL executed in the whole window), I can't respond "systemic 2-5+ candle lateness" as a blanket, precise number. What I can say with confidence: **the scan-suppression-during-open-position pattern is 100% consistent across every trade checked**, and it is the dominant, best-evidenced mechanism behind the specific complaint trade. The soft-gate dwell pattern (SMART-GUARD/B-GRADE/momentum-slowdown/A+ demotion chains) is present in roughly half the sample (3–4 of 8) and produces comparable 6–8 candle delays through a different mechanism (repeated rejection, not blindness).

## 4. Dominant module

Two candidates, both real, operating differently:

1. **Position-open scan suppression (architectural, not a "gate")** — confirmed in 8/8 trades, zero exceptions. This is the mechanism behind the user's specific complaint trade. It isn't a blocker with a reasonKey; it's an absence of any evaluation at all while a trade is live.
2. **Repeated soft-gate rejection cycles** (SMART-GUARD fast-TF confirmation, "momentum slowdown (close in opposite 30% of last 3-bar range)", B-GRADE QUALITY BLOCK, A+ EVIDENCE DEMOTION) — present in 3-4 of 8 trades, each re-firing on 5-minute cycles for 15-40 minutes before an eventually-graded-A candidate fires.

**Honest read given the sample size (8 trades, 1 in the SELL direction): I cannot cleanly rank these as "the" single dominant cause across all trading** — they show up in different trades and are mechanically distinct. For the *specific* BUY-SL-SELL trade the user flagged, mechanism #1 is unambiguously the cause (0 gate rejections intervened; the delay is 100% attributable to the EA not looking at the market for 40 minutes). For the broader pattern of "bot seems to enter late," mechanism #2 is at least as visible in this small sample and matches the soft/hard blocker taxonomy documented in the two prior audits (`SMART-GUARD`, `B-GRADE QUALITY BLOCK`, `A+ EVIDENCE DEMOTION` all appear on the prior audits' "blocked signal" tables too).

## 5. Could the flagged trade have entered earlier without violating hard safety?

For the SELL leg specifically: **the EA had no opportunity to enter earlier, because it wasn't looking.** There is no hard-safety rejection (spread, news, structural contradiction, risk limit) logged anywhere between the BUY's open and the SELL's open — there are no logged decision cycles at all in that window to have hard- or soft-blocked anything. This is categorically different from the pattern in the two prior audits (where specific candidates were seen and then soft-blocked for reasons of varying legitimacy) — here, nothing was evaluated, so nothing was blocked, hard or soft.

For the two pre-existing tracked SELL candidates that were live before the BUY (`1783499313` blocked 08:28 with B-GRADE QUALITY BLOCK, `1783501814` blocked 09:10 with SMART-GUARD): both are **soft** rejections ("failed stricter fast XAU confirmation" / "weak fast-timeframe confirmation... M30 NEUTRAL, H1 OK") — not hard safety gates. But both also show `wouldSL1R=Y` in their own forward tracking, meaning taking either of those two specific signals literally would **not** have produced a clean win — so "unblock them" is not a clean fix on this evidence. The fix, if there is one, is not about the blocking logic for those two signals; it's about why the EA had a live BUY position open at all, against a 9-hour prevailing bearish structure, on a single overbought spike bar.

## 6. Data limitations (explicit)

- **No MT5 Terminal Journal `Print()` logs exist on this machine for this EA/period.** Confirmed by directory search (no `MQL5/Logs/`) and by content-checking every `.log` file found (all are LiveUpdate/shutdown service logs, none are EA output). The task's `ADAPTIVE-DIRECTION | HTF Bias | Active Direction | Reason` log line format does not appear anywhere in the jsonl either (0 matches on 4 different search strings). This audit's "recognition" and "earliest evidence" columns are therefore built from `BLOCKED`/`BLOCK_CHECK`/`MARKET_SNAPSHOT` proxies, not the literal state variable the task asked for — flagged in every relevant section above, not silently substituted.
- **Zero visibility into intra-trade price action.** The EA logs no `MARKET_SNAPSHOT`/`BLOCK_CHECK` events while any position is open (confirmed 8/8, zero exceptions). For the flagged trade this creates a genuine, un-fillable 40-minute gap in the record — I bounded the reversal's start between the last bullish read (09:25:15) and the first bearish read (10:06:20) rather than guessing a specific bar.
- **Trade #3 (9448484106) and #6 (9453717930)** could not be given a precise "earliest valid evidence" timestamp with full confidence — the surrounding `extra` text was either truncated in this pass or the price action was genuinely ambiguous (chop, not a clean reversal bar). Flagged in the table rather than forced to a number.
- **Sample size is 8 executed trades over ~35 hours, only 1 of which is a SELL.** Any "systemic" claim about direction-recognition delay is necessarily provisional — this is not enough data to fit a distribution, only enough to say the position-open blackout is consistent and the soft-gate-dwell pattern recurs.
- **`Market personality` field** (RANGE/MOMENTUM_CONT/STRONG_TREND/etc.) was read from the daily Scorecard `.txt` file by exact `HH:MM | SETUP DIR` match where available; not every decision cycle in the jsonl has a corresponding Scorecard line, so personality is only reported where an exact match existed.
- This audit did not re-derive real M5 OHLC from MT5's binary `.hc` cache files (the same undocumented-format obstacle the prior opposite-direction audit hit and explicitly declined to guess at) — all prices here are the EA's own logged `MARKET_SNAPSHOT`/`OPEN`/`CLOSE` values, which is genuine data but not a continuous independent price series.
